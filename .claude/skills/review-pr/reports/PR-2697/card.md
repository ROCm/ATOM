## ATOM PR #2697 — fix(dp): a decoding rank replays the group's prefill token-graph bucket

**On a DP-attention step where one rank prefills while a peer decodes (prefill delayer off), every rank of the group — the decoding one included — is now padded to the smallest recorded prefill token-graph bucket holding the group's largest row count and replays that prefill recording, so the recorded MoE DP collectives meet the shapes they were recorded with instead of hanging the group.**
